# Koral — Marketing Plan

> **Purpose:** Outline how Koral reaches, acquires, and retains users through positioning, channel strategy, content, and community-led growth.

---

## 1. Positioning

### 1.1 Elevator Pitch

> **Koral is the calm, focused note-taking app for people who think clearly and capture freely.**

Organize notes with bi-directional links, search instantly, sync safely through plain files, and keep your data local-first—no clutter, no lock-in, no telemetry by default.

### 1.2 Brand Personality

| Attribute | Expression |
|---|---|
| Clear | Simple language; no jargon |
| Calm | Quiet confidence; no fear-based marketing |
| Inclusive | Accessible by default; RTL + i18n |
| Open | Plain formats, portable data |
| Respectful | Privacy-first; no tracking |

### 1.3 Positioning Map

```
Low Power ←——————————————————————————————→ High Power
Low Simplicity | Notion / Obsidian | Koral | Apple Notes | High Simplicity
```

**Interpretation:** Koral occupies the underserved middle: structured knowledge management without steep setup or cloud lock-in.

## 2. Target Audiences

### 2.1 Primary Segments

| Segment | Demographics | Psychographics | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge Workers | 25–50, professionals, researchers | Fast capture + reliable sync + search | SEO, LinkedIn, Twitter/X |
| Students & Academics | 18–30, university/PhD | Offline access, affordability, citation linking | Reddit, TikTok, university groups |
| Developers / Technical Users | 22–45, engineers, IT admins | Markdown-first, local files, API access | GitHub, Hacker News, Dev.to |
| Privacy-Conscious Individuals | 25–55, journalers, activists | Local-first, E2EE, zero-knowledge | Mastodon, newsletters, forums |
| Accessibility-First Users | All ages, screen-reader/keyboard users | WCAG 2.2 AA, semantic UI, RTL | Accessibility communities, advocacy groups |

### 2.2 Personas

- **Jordan, 29, Software Engineer** — Wants Markdown notes, local search, and Git-friendly exports. Uses Koral for project notes and RFCs.
- **Sam, 38, Graduate Student** — Needs offline access during fieldwork, citation linking, and export to PDF/Word for advisors.
- **Alex, 45, Privacy Advocate** — Uses Koral because it’s local-first, E2EE optional, and has no telemetry.

## 3. Competitive Messaging

### 3.1 Messaging Matrix

| Situation | Competitor Mention | Koral Response |
|---|---|---|
| “Is this like Notion?” | Notion | “Notion is a database with notes. Koral is a note app that thinks like you do—fast, local, and link-first.” |
| “I use Obsidian” | Obsidian | “Obsidian is powerful but needs plugins and paid sync. Koral gives you linking, search, and sync out of the box.” |
| “Apple Notes is enough” | Apple Notes | “Apple Notes is great for quick capture. Koral adds backlinks, structured search, and cross-platform sync.” |
| “I don’t trust cloud apps” | — | “Koral is local-first. Your notes live on your device first; sync is optional and encrypted.” |

### 3.2 Taglines by Channel

- **App Store:** “Think clearly. Capture freely. Own your notes.”
- **Social:** “The note app that respects your mind and your privacy.”
- **SEO:** “Local-first note-taking app with bi-directional links and plain Markdown.”
- **Developer:** “Markdown, local-first, agent-ready. The note app for builders.”

## 4. Channel Strategy

### 4.1 Owned Channels

- **App / Web**
  - Optimize for “local-first notes,” “Markdown note app,” “privacy-first PKM”
  - Keep onboarding minimal; show capture speed
- **Website / Blog**
  - Landing pages: “Local-first notes,” “Bi-directional links,” “Plain Markdown”
  - Content: privacy, PKM workflows, accessibility
- **Email Newsletter**
  - Monthly: feature updates, workflow tips, community highlights

### 4.2 Earned Channels

- **Press & Media**
  - Pitch to productivity editors at *The Verge*, *Ars Technica*, *Hacker News*
  - Angles: “local-first note app,” “privacy-first PKM,” “agent-ready notes”
- **Communities**
  - Reddit: r/notetaking, r/selfhosted, r/privacytools
  - Discord/Slack: PKM communities, developer tooling groups

### 4.3 Paid Channels

- **ASO/SEO**
  - Target keywords: “local-first notes,” “Markdown PKM,” “privacy note app”
- **Social**
  - TikTok/Reels: 60-second workflow demos
  - Twitter/X: developer/PR updates
- **Influencer Seeding**
  - Productivity creators, privacy advocates, academic tech reviewers

## 5. Content Plan

### 5.1 Editorial Pillars

1. **Workflow** — capture, organize, link, export

### 5.2 90-Day Calendar

| Month | Focus | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch prep | Landing page, docs, demo video |

## 6. Early Access / Community

- **Scout Beta**: 500 slots; signup via website
- **Ambassador Tiers**: Scout / Guide / Ranger based on contribution
- **Referral Loop**: invite friends → extended sync/quota
- **Community Rituals**: weekly workflow threads, monthly changelog AMA

---

## 7. KPIs

- Activation rate ≥ 60%
- Conversion ≥ 4.5%
- Churn ≤ 6%
- NPS ≥ 50
- App/web rating ≥ 4.7

---

## 8. Budget

- Bootstrap-friendly early phase: focus on owned content + community
- Paid acquisition only after product-market fit signals
- Target: <$5k spend in first 90 days
