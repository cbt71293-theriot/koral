# Naming Rationale — Koral

## The Name

**Koral**

Pronounced: /ˈkɔːrəl/ — rhymes with "moral"

---

## Why "Koral"?

### 1. Distinctiveness

"Koral" is not an existing major note-taking application. It avoids the saturated "note," "pad," "ink," and "jot" namespace while still feeling relevant to the act of recording thoughts.

### 2. Natural Metaphor

The spelling evokes **coral** — organic, structured, and growing. Just as coral builds complex, beautiful structures from tiny units, Koral helps users build rich knowledge from small, individual notes.

### 3. Memorability

Four letters. Two syllables. Strong consonant start (K) with a smooth vowel-open ending. Easy to say, easy to spell, easy to recall.

### 4. Brandability

- Works as a verb ("koraling that idea") and a noun.
- Scales to sub-brands: Koral Coral (team tier), Koral Tide (sync), Koral Reef (community).
- Short enough for app icons, badges, and social handles.

### 5. Emotional Resonance

"Coral" suggests calm waters, discovery, and quiet depth — aligning with Koral's positioning as a focused, uncluttered note-taking experience.

---

## Alternatives Considered

| Name | Reason Rejected |
|---|---|
| Notely | Too close to existing products; generic |
| Jotter | Overused in stationery space |
| Memoria | Sounds clinical; hard to spell for some |
| Scribe | Implies writing, not organizing |
| Vello | Unclear pronunciation; weak metaphor |
