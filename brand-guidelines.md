# Brand Guidelines — Koral

## Overview

**Koral** is a modern note-taking application designed for clarity, speed, and creative flow. These guidelines ensure every touchpoint—product UI, marketing assets, packaging, and social presence—feels cohesive and unmistakably Koral.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

| Attribute | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Koral |
| **Category** | Note-taking & thought organization |
| **Positioning** | The calm, focused alternative to cluttered note apps |
| **Core promise** | Think clearly. Capture freely. |

---

## 2. Logo

### Primary Lockup

The Koral wordmark is the primary brand identifier. It is set in a custom geometric sans-serif, all lowercase, with a subtle rounded treatment on the terminals of the **k**, **o**, and **l** to evoke organic coral forms.

### Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "k" around all sides of the logo. Do not crowd the logo with other elements.

### Minimum Size

- Print / PDF: 18 pt type equivalent
- Digital: 48 px wide
- Favicon / app icon: 32 px minimum

### Misuse Rules

- Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the logo.
- Do not change the logo color outside the approved palette.
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or bevels.
- Do not place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid backing shape.
- Do not spell the name with a capital K or add punctuation.

## 3. Color Palette

### Primary Colors

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coral Core** | `#FF6B5B` | `#FF6B5B` | Primary actions, hero accents, app icon |
| **Deep Ocean** | `#0F2B3C` | `#0F2B3C` | Headings, primary text, dark UI surfaces |
| **Foam White** | `#FAF9F6` | `#FAF9F6` | Backgrounds, cards, light UI surfaces |
| **Tide Teal** | `#3AA8A1` | `#3AA8A1` | Secondary actions, links, success states |

### Secondary / Supporting Colors

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sand** | `#E8E4DF` | `#E8E4DF` | Dividers, disabled states, subtle borders |
| **Driftwood** | `#8C7E72` | `#8C7E72` | Captions, helper text, placeholders |
| **Kelp Green** | `#7FA650` | `#7FA650` | Positive metrics, tags, highlights |
| **Abyss** | `#061820` | `#061820` | Footer areas, dark mode base |
| **Pearl** | `#FFFFFF` | `#FFFFFF` | Card backgrounds, elevated surfaces |

### Accessibility

- All text/background combinations must pass WCAG 2.1 AA (minimum 4.5:1 ratio for body text).
- **Deep Ocean** on **Foam White** = 12.6:1 ✓
- **Coral Core** on **Foam White** = 3.8:1 — use only for large text (≥18 pt) or decorative non-text elements.
- **Tide Teal** on **Foam White** = 3.2:1 — pair with bold weight for body copy.

## 4. Typography

### Primary Typeface

**Inter** — a versatile, highly legible geometric sans-serif.

- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)
- Preferred for: UI chrome, body copy, headings, marketing materials
- Source: [fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)

### Display / Headlines

**Plus Jakarta Sans** — slightly warmer, more expressive geometric sans-serif.

- Weights: SemiBold (600), Bold (700), ExtraBold (800)
- Preferred for: Hero headlines, splash screens, large marketing typography
- Source: [fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans)

### Monospace

**JetBrains Mono**

- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500)
- Preferred for: Code blocks, keyboard shortcuts, technical annotations
- Source: [fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono)

### Type Scale

| Level | Size | Weight | Line Height | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | 48 px | 700 | 1.1 | Page hero, splash |
| H2 | 32 px | 700 | 1.2 | Section headings |
| H3 | 24 px | 600 | 1.3 | Card titles |
| Body | 16 px | 400 | 1.6 | Reading copy, notes |
| Small | 14 px | 400 | 1.5 | Captions, meta |
| Tiny | 12 px | 500 | 1.4 | Labels, tags |

## 5. Tone of Voice

Koral speaks with clarity, warmth, and quiet confidence.

### Personality Traits

1. **Clear over clever** — Say exactly what you mean. Strip jargon.
2. **Warm, not sappy** — Approachable like a trusted friend, not a salesperson.
3. **Focused** — Respect attention. Short sentences. Active voice.
4. **Quietly confident** — No hype, no exclamation marks. Let the product speak.

### Do & Don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Use plain language | Use marketing buzzwords |
| Write short sentences | Use walls of text |
| Be encouraging, not pushy | Overpromise or overclaim |
| Respect the user's time | Pad copy with fluff |

### Example Phrases

- "Your thoughts, organized."
- "A calmer way to take notes."
- "Capture now. Find later."
- "Koral stays out of your way."

## 7. UI / Product Usage Rules

- **Buttons**: Use **Coral Core** for primary CTAs, **Tide Teal** for secondary actions. Text on Coral Core should be white or Deep Ocean for contrast.
- **Backgrounds**: Default to **Foam White** in light mode, **Abyss** in dark mode.
- **Text**: Body copy in **Deep Ocean** (light mode) or **Foam White** (dark mode). Never use Coral Core for long-form reading text.
- **Borders / dividers**: **Sand** or **Driftwood** at 20–40% opacity.
- **Focus states**: Use **Tide Teal** outline (2 px, 4 px radius).
- **Error states**: A warm red `#E05252` (not Coral Core) reserved for destructive / error messaging only.

---

## 8. Marketing & Social

- Profile pictures and avatars: Use the coral circle mark (logo inside a solid `#FF6B5B` circle, mark in white).
- Cover images: Gradient from **Coral Core** to **Tide Teal** on **Foam White** or **Deep Ocean**.
- Social captions: Keep under 280 characters. Match Koral tone — clear, warm, concise.
